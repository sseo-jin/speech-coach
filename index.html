1 Paste your script

Your script works like an answer key. Leave a blank line between parts (intro, body 1, body 2…). Each part becomes one cue card.

2 Record your voice

Record right here, or upload a file you recorded before (m4a, mp3, wav…). A new take replaces the old one.

    3 Check your score

    With the pronunciation check on, your latest take is sent to Microsoft Azure to be scored. Everything else stays in this browser.

    Repeat this process. Every time you analyze this script, your Ready score and the sentences you got stuck on are saved to your account.

    Sentences you got stuck on this time, or keep missing, are written out in full, in big letters. The rest show just the first words.
