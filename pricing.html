Questions

Do my recordings leave my computer?

No. Speech-to-text runs in your browser. Only when you turn on the pronunciation check is your latest take sent to Microsoft Azure to be scored.

How many times should I record?

About three times. A sentence counts as “stuck” only when it trips you up in two takes or more, so one bad take never fools the coach.
