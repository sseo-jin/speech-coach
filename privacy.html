Your privacy

What we keep, what we send, and what never leaves your computer.

Saved to your account

Your email, your password (stored only as a hash), and when you signed up and last logged in. For each practice run: your Ready score, how many takes, how many stuck sentences, and when. Your script is saved only as a code made from its words, never as text. You can see only your own scores.

Sent to Microsoft, only if you turn on the pronunciation check

Your latest take and your script go to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to be scored.

Kept only in this browser

Your script, your target time, your transcripts and your last result. Your recordings stay on your computer. Speech-to-text runs in this browser.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads libraries from jsDelivr and the speech model from Hugging Face, which may keep standard access logs such as your IP address.

Delete your data

Use “Reset all my scores” in step 5 to delete every score in your account.
